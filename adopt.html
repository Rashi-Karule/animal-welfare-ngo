Adopt an Indie Dog

Give a rescued dog a forever home. Adoption saves lives.

Why Adopt an Indie?

Indie (Indian native) dogs are resilient, adaptable, and deeply loyal. They are well-suited to our climate, often healthier than many pedigrees, and full of love. When you adopt, you save a life and make space for another rescue.

  • Resilient and suited to local climate
  • Loyal and loving companions
  • Often fewer genetic health issues
  • You directly save a life and support rescue work
